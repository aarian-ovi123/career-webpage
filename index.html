Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

Overview

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) is a senior-level executive responsible for overseeing an organization's information, cybersecurity, and technological security.

Why does it interests me?

I am interested in cybersecurity, and I work in cybersecurity with a mentor. Another reason is that this is a highly regarded, highly positioned role.

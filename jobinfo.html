Description

A CISO is a senior executive tasked with overseeing an organization's information security, which encompasses data protection, network security, and safeguarding computer systems from cyber threats. This role requires the creation and enforcement of security policies and procedures, management of security teams, and collaboration with other executives to ensure security is prioritized. A CISO should possess a strong understanding of information security technologies and be able to effectively communicate with individuals across different levels of technical expertise and professional backgrounds.
